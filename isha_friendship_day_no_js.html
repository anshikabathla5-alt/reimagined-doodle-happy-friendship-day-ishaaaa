Chapter 01

Dear Isha,

I don't really know how to make a fight disappear with one perfect sentence, so I won't pretend I can. I don't want this to be a dramatic apology where I say a few pretty things and suddenly everything is magically fixed.

The truth is much simpler: I care about you. I care about us. And that is exactly why our fights hurt. When someone matters to you, even small things can suddenly feel enormous.

I've been thinking about all the little things that make us us — the random conversations, jokes nobody else would understand, the moments when one look says everything, and the complete nonsense we somehow manage to turn into memories.

And honestly? I miss you. I miss us. I miss being able to send you something stupid without thinking twice. I miss laughing with you. I miss that comfortable feeling of knowing I had my person somewhere in all the chaos.

I'm sorry for the things I said, the things I did, and the things I could have handled better. I'm not asking you to forget how you felt. I'm just hoping we can talk properly — not to decide who was right, but because this friendship is worth more than winning an argument.

One bad chapter does not suddenly make the whole story bad. And I refuse to believe everything beautiful we've shared is smaller than one stupid fight.

So on Friendship Day, I'm saying it properly: I love you, Isha. Platonically. Ridiculously. Annoyingly. And with a heart that still has a reserved seat for you. 🫶

And yes, you're still my favourite idiot. Unfortunately, that position remains permanently occupied.

There's one last thing… 💗
I don't need us to be perfect. I just want us to be real enough to fight, talk, forgive, laugh, and somehow find our way back. That's my favourite thing about us. Now please stop making me emotional and come back. 😭💗
