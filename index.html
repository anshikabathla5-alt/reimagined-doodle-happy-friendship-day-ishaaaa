Chapter 01

Dear Isha,

I don't really know how to make a fight disappear with one perfect sentence, so I won't pretend I can. I also don't want this to be one of those dramatic apologies where I say a few nice words and act like everything is instantly okay.

The truth is much simpler. I care about you. I care about us. And that's exactly why our fights hurt as much as they do. Because when someone matters to you, even tiny things can feel huge.

I've been thinking about all the silly little things that make us us — the random conversations, the jokes nobody else would understand, the moments where one look says everything, the absolute nonsense we somehow manage to turn into a memory.

And honestly? I miss you. I miss us. I miss being able to send you something stupid without overthinking it. I miss laughing with you. I miss the comfort of knowing that somewhere in all this chaos, I had my person.

I'm sorry for the things I said, the things I did, and the things I could have handled better. I'm not asking you to forget how you felt. I'm just hoping we can get to a place where we can talk, understand each other, forgive what needs forgiving, and choose our friendship again.

Because one bad chapter does not suddenly make the whole story bad. And I refuse to believe that everything beautiful we have shared is smaller than one stupid fight.

So, on Friendship Day, this is me saying it properly: I love you, Isha. Platonically. Ridiculously. Annoyingly. And with a heart that still has a reserved seat for you. 🫶

And yes, you're still my favourite idiot. Unfortunately, that position remains permanently occupied.

I don't need us to be perfect. I just want us to be real enough to fight, talk, forgive, laugh, and somehow find our way back. That's my favourite thing about us. Now please stop making me emotional and come back. 😭💗
